Firelight — Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

Firelight collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no account with us — because there is no “us” to have an account with. Firelight is a client for a server you run. It talks to that server and to nothing else unless you explicitly turn on one of the optional features below.

The App Store privacy label for Firelight is Data Not Collected, and that is the whole truth rather than a technicality.

What stays on your device

  • Your server’s address and your credentials. Stored in the iOS Keychain, in a shared access group so the app, its widgets and the share extension reach the same tokens. Server addresses and tokens are redacted before anything is written to a log or a diagnostic export.
  • A cache of your library — titles, authors, cover art, chapters and progress — so the app works without a connection.
  • Your listening and reading positions, written to a local journal before they are sent anywhere, so a lost connection cannot lose your place.
  • Downloaded books, in the app’s own container.

None of this is transmitted to anyone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Firelight sends, and where

To your Audiobookshelf server, and only the one you configured: your sign-in, requests for your library and files, and your playback positions. That is the whole point of the app. Your server is yours; what it logs is between you and it.

Firelight has no server of its own. There is no telemetry endpoint, no “phone home” on launch, and no third-party SDK embedded in the app.

Optional features that involve someone else

Each is off until you turn it on, and each is the only thing that sends data anywhere other than your own server.

  • iCloud sync for highlights. Passages you highlight and notes you write sync through your private iCloud database so they follow you between your devices. That is Apple’s infrastructure and your private storage; the developer cannot read it. Your library, positions and downloads are not synced.
  • Hardcover. If you paste a Hardcover API token into Settings, Firelight sends the title, author and reading status of books you finish so your shelves there stay current. Nothing is sent until you supply a token, and removing it stops it. Hardcover’s own policy governs what they do with it.
  • Send to Kindle. The file goes through your own mail or share sheet to the Kindle address you entered. Amazon’s terms govern what happens after that.
  • Tips. Processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple does not share your payment details with the developer. Tips unlock nothing; the whole app is free.
  • Catch Me Up. Summaries are produced by Apple’s on-device model on supported devices. The text never leaves your phone, and the model is never given anything past where you have read.

Children

Kids Mode simplifies the library and can be locked behind your device passcode. It changes what is shown; it collects nothing, because nothing is collected from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here and the date above changes with it. Firelight is open source — if you would rather verify than trust, the code is the policy: every network call the app can make lives in ABSEndpoints.swift and HardcoverClient.swift.

Firelight is free and open source, released under the MIT licence. Questions or corrections: open an issue on the repository.
